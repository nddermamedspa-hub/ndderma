## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can clients book without membership?**
A: Yes! Memberships are optional. All services available for one-time booking at full price.

**Q: What if a member cancels their membership?**
A: Members can cancel anytime (monthly plans) or with 30-day notice (annual). They lose future discount but keep appointment history.

**Q: How are complimentary add-ons tracked?**
A: Manually tracked in Square notes or via custom Square fields. Can be automated with Square APIs if needed later.

**Q: Can members pause their membership?**
A: Yes. Configure in Square to allow pausing for 1-month without charge.

**Q: How do members access their portal?**
A: Square generates login link. Embed on your website in a "Members Only" section.

**Q: What payment methods does Square accept?**
A: All major cards (Visa, MC, Amex, Discover), Apple Pay, Google Pay, Square Cash.
