## 📱 Responsive Considerations

- **Desktop:** Full navigation, 4-card grid, 3-column footer
- **Tablet:** Condensed nav, 2-column grid, 2-column footer
- **Mobile:** Hamburger menu, stacked cards, single-column layout, larger touch targets

---

## 🎨 Design Notes

- **Color Palette:** Use soft beige, gold, black, white from brand assets
- **Typography:** Luxury serif for headlines, clean sans-serif for body
- **Imagery:** High-quality, professional, aspirational clinic/treatment photos
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace for premium feel
- **Buttons:** Consistent styling, high contrast for accessibility
