## ❓ Common Questions Answered

**Q: Will this work with Elementor?**
A: Yes! Square Appointments widget integrates directly with Elementor Pro.

**Q: Can clients book without membership?**
A: Yes! Memberships are optional. All services available for one-time booking.

**Q: How much does Square cost?**
A: No monthly fee. Square charges per transaction (typically 2.9% + $0.30 per card).

**Q: Can staff see bookings in real-time?**
A: Yes! Square Dashboard updates in real-time.

**Q: What if a member cancels?**
A: They can cancel anytime (monthly plans) or with 30-day notice (annual).
