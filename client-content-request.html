Website Content Request

ND Derma Med Spa · For design & development handoff · March 2026

This checklist lists everything needed to finish the site content, imagery, and booking setup. Please return files and copy using the formats noted so production can move without rework.

1. Photography & imagery

Hero & atmosphere

  • 1–3 wide hero images (clinic interior, treatment ambiance, or brand lifestyle). Landscape, high resolution; aim for ~2400px+ wide minimum.
  • Optional: additional section backgrounds for Services page hero and key banners (same quality).

Before & after gallery

  • Minimum ~10 before/after pairs for a full gallery page; homepage preview uses 3–4 pairs.
  • Each pair: clearly matched lighting/angle where possible; label with treatment type (and optional timeframe).
  • Written photo release / consent on file for every identifiable patient (required for web use).

Clinic, team, and details

  • Optional: staff or provider headshots (consistent background, high res) for About or team sections.
  • Optional: equipment / treatment room shots for category cards or service areas.

Technical

  • Preferred: JPG or PNG at original camera resolution; avoid heavy compression.
  • Name files clearly (e.g. hero-clinic-01.jpg, ba-laser-pigment-01-before.jpg).

2. Testimonials & social proof

  • 3–5 real testimonials for the “Client Love” section (short quotes work well).
  • For each: exact quote, and optionally first name + last initial and city or treatment type (only what they approve).
  • If you want names withheld, provide initials or “Verified client” style attribution per your policy.

3. Copy & business information

Footer & contact (required)

  • Legal business name as it should appear on the site
  • Phone (primary booking line), email, full street address
  • Instagram (full URL or @handle) and any other social links to display
  • Hours of operation (if shown on Contact)

“Why choose us” blocks

  • Four short blurbs (roughly 2–4 sentences each) for: Advanced Technology, Natural-Looking Results, Personalized Plans, Luxury Experience — or your preferred headings if you change them.

Signature packages (homepage)

  • Confirm or rewrite names/descriptions for the three featured packages.
  • Public prices or “from $X” if you display pricing on the site.
Service descriptions for individual treatments are already drafted in project documentation; you mainly need to confirm accuracy, disclaimers, and pricing (see below).

4. Pricing & Square / booking

  • Retail price per service (or “consultation required”) for every service offered online.
  • Deposit rules if different from default (e.g. 50% deposit vs full pay).
  • Membership tiers (Bronze / Silver / Gold): final monthly or annual prices and any rules you want shown publicly.
  • Durations and which staff or rooms apply, if relevant for the booking system.

5. Brand & legal

  • Final logo files (vector PDF/EPS/SVG plus PNG with transparent background).
  • Confirm tagline: “Where elegance meets aesthetics.” (or supply replacement).
  • Draft or final Privacy Policy and Terms links/text for footer when you are ready to publish.

6. Technical / launch (your team or IT)

  • Domain name and DNS access (or registrar login for whoever points the domain).
  • Hosting preference or existing host credentials if developers deploy for you.
